# Decision: log unfixed findings by group in write-once files, with the sweep's rulings in one append-only file

Issue #200 (Heavy). The human approved this record with the groomed body (2026-11-22 grooming, D-200-1 to D-200-4 answered with option 1; `approved` label covering body sha256 `e30ebd68e003fb555ea789cfe77933ed88a793a5398f2b13680503d2818b71e4`, Orchestrator Claim comment on #200, 2026-11-22). Copied here from the issue body's `## Decision record`.

- **Decision:** An entry's state lives in one append-only file, `_docs/records/findings-rulings.jsonl` (rulings `issued`, `dropped`, `kept`, `regroup`), so the per-issue findings files stay write-once (D-200-1). An entry's age is the number of sweep entries named later than the file's `logged` stamp, or than its last `kept` ruling (D-200-2, D-200-4). QA names an entry's group and reuses a slug listed by `make findings`; the orchestrator copies it unchanged; the sweep may regroup by a `regroup` ruling (D-200-3). A new read-only script, `findings.py` (`make findings`), validates every entry with `check-format.py`'s `check_finding`, refuses a bad file or ruling by name, and prints the open entries by group with age, debt totals and the two flags.
- **Reason:** The log has to answer "what is still open?" without the files changing, because the findings are a record of what a role reported at close. A rulings file is also what socboard settled on (`findings-index.jsonl`). A stamp in the file and a count of sweep entries need no git history, so the age is the same in a shallow or squashed clone.
- **Alternatives:** (1) A `status` edited into each findings file: one place to read, but the files are no longer write-once and each sweep edits many of them. (2) Deleting issued and dropped entries: the history of what was dropped is lost. (3) Age from the file's first commit in git: no new field, but it breaks in a shallow or squashed clone. (4) The orchestrator regroups at close (as by hand on #199) or only the sweep groups: QA's comment and the log then disagree, or `FINDINGS_GROUP_AT` can't act between sweeps. (5) Keep as permanent: an entry nobody revisits is the loss the log exists to prevent.
- **Trade-offs:** Two places are read to know an entry's state (the file and the rulings), and the script must refuse a ruling for an unknown id. Age counts sweeps, so with no sweep entry every age is 0 and nothing is flagged `keep or drop` until the first sweep. A group name depends on QA reusing a slug, and a near-duplicate slug is caught only by a human or a `regroup` ruling, not by the script. A ruling's `at` is a sweep entry's name time, so a renamed entry changes ages.
- **Evidence:** the grooming comment on #200 (the human's answers of 2026-11-22: option 1 of D-200-1 to D-200-4); `_docs/planning/kit-1.11/workflow-review.md` sections 3.5, 4.2 and 12; `filed.md` grooming notes A, C and E; `_docs/records/findings/196.json` to `199.json` (26 entries; the two slugs for F-199-2); `socboard/_docs/records/` (`findings-index.jsonl` and the `checker-looser-than-intent` group); `agent-kit/scripts/sweep-due.py` (the sweep entry name pattern).

Design note from the body: rulings are an append-only file, not edits to the findings files (D-200-1); age is counted in sweep entries from a `logged` stamp (D-200-2); QA proposes the group and the sweep may regroup by ruling (D-200-3); `kept` restarts age (D-200-4).
