# Decision: the Engineer reports against the numbered criteria in four fixed forms, checked by a doc test and by QA's reading

Issue #201 (Heavy). The human approved this record with the groomed body (2026-11-22 grooming, D-201-1 and D-201-2 answered with option 1; `approved` label covering body sha256 `ecee4746d561501919ce1938f03e595bac96b29fa21e6cf20d6b0c46a41666cc`, Orchestrator Claim comment on #201, 2026-11-22). Copied here from the issue body's `## Decision record`.

- **Decision:** The Engineer's per-criterion list is part of its issue comment, in four fixed forms (`done (<evidence>)`, `not done`, `not checked (<reason>)`, `challenged (<reason>)`) defined once in `procedures/formats.md`. A doc test checks that the forms are named there and that the Engineer's and QA's role files point to them; QA checks the list by reading it and says on its own `Criteria` line where it disagrees. No script reads the Engineer's comment, and `check-format` gets no new KIND (D-201-1, option 1). The issue stays Heavy (D-201-2, option 1).
- **Reason:** The disagreement the list exists to expose is cheap for QA to see by reading, and QA already re-checks every criterion without trusting the Engineer's claims. A defined form puts the disagreement in one place (a line per criterion) before the QA run, and `challenged` gives the Engineer's early "can't be met as written" question a visible place in the list.
- **Alternatives:** (1) A new `check-format KIND=engineer` that reads the issue dump and refuses a missing criterion, a repeated id or a `done` with no evidence: mechanical, but a live script change and effort E for a gap QA already sees. (2) The list inside the role return's `Detail:` file: no new place to read, but the return is a fixed ten-line block and the issue comment would not show the list. (3) Restating the forms in each role file: shorter to read, but three copies to keep in step. (4) Lowering the issue to Standard: skips the record and the second launch, but an agent may not lower a Heavy issue and the role files are what every session loads.
- **Trade-offs:** Nothing refuses a list with a missing line or a `done` with no evidence; only QA's reading and the human's review catch it, and the doc test checks the wording of the rule, not any comment. `challenged` adds a fourth form an Engineer might overuse to avoid hard criteria; QA's rule that an unresolved `challenged` goes to the human limits that. The role files grow by a few lines each, which the sweep tracks.
